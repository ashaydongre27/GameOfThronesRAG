Episode 1: The Wars to Come
About twenty-five years earlier in the Westerlands, a young Cersei Lannister and her friend Melara Hetherspoon sneak through the woods to a hut belonging to a woods witch named Maggy. Melara is scared and wants to turn back, but Cersei pushes her on. Inside, Cersei demands her future be told. Maggy hesitates until Cersei threatens her, then takes a drop of Cersei’s blood. Maggy answers three questions. Cersei will not marry the prince but will marry the king and become queen. The king will have twenty children, but Cersei will have three. Gold will be their crowns and gold their shrouds. Cersei will be queen until a younger, more beautiful queen takes everything she holds dear.

In the present day in King’s Landing, Cersei arrives at the Great Sept of Baelor to pay respects to her dead father Tywin. She blames Jaime for freeing Tyrion and causing Tywin’s death. At the wake Lancel Lannister, now a devout Sparrow, asks her forgiveness for their past affair and for getting Robert drunk the day he died. Cersei mocks him and denies any knowledge of Robert’s death. Elsewhere Loras Tyrell lies in bed with the prostitute Olyvar. They notice a birthmark on Loras’s thigh that looks like Dorne. Margaery walks in, complains about the lack of discretion, and sends Olyvar away. Loras says his preferences are an open secret and that with Tywin dead no one will force Cersei to marry him. Margaery replies only “perhaps.”

In Pentos Tyrion climbs out of the crate Varys used to smuggle him. Varys tells him that he and Illyrio once plotted to restore House Targaryen but failed. Tyrion is bitter and wants only to drink himself to death. Varys snaps him out of it, saying Westeros needs a strong yet gentle ruler who can please the people and control the nobles. When Tyrion says no such man exists, Varys notes he never said the ruler would be a man. He offers Tyrion a choice: drink himself to death or travel to Meereen to meet Daenerys. Tyrion agrees to go.

In Meereen the Unsullied topple the giant golden harpy statue from the Great Pyramid to show the old ways are finished. An Unsullied named White Rat goes to a brothel and pays a woman named Vala simply to hold him. While he lies there a Son of the Harpy slits his throat and leaves a golden harpy mask. Daenerys is shown the mask and orders Grey Worm to find those responsible. She has White Rat buried in the Temple of the Graces as a statement. Hizdahr zo Loraq and Daario return from Yunkai. The Wise Masters will hand power to a mixed council of former slaves and masters if Daenerys reopens the fighting pits. She refuses. Daario later urges her to reconsider, saying the pits taught him to fight and led him to her. He notes the irony of a dragon queen with no dragons after Drogon has been missing for weeks. Daenerys visits the chained dragons Viserion and Rhaegal in the catacombs. They try to attack her and she flees.

In the Vale Littlefinger and Sansa watch young Robin Arryn spar poorly. Littlefinger receives a message and hides it. Lord Yohn Royce will foster Robin but doubts the boy’s skill. Littlefinger told Royce they were going to the Fingers, but they head west instead. Sansa asks if they fear Royce; Littlefinger says he trusts no one who saw them and is taking her somewhere the Lannisters will never find her. Nearby Brienne tells Podrick she wants no one following her. Pod reminds her of her oath to Jaime to find the Stark girls. Brienne says Arya rejected her and Pod is now safe, so they can part. Littlefinger’s carriage passes them without Brienne realizing who is inside.

At the Wall Jon trains Olly until Melisandre fetches him for Stannis. On the ride up the Wall Melisandre unsettles Jon with questions. Stannis plans to retake the North from Roose Bolton and wants the Free Folk to fight for him. He needs Jon to convince Mance Rayder to bend the knee; in return the wildlings will get freedom and land. Jon tells Mance. Mance refuses because kneeling would show weakness to his people. His punishment is death by fire. On the night of the execution Mance stands before Stannis, cannot kneel, and wishes him luck in the wars to come. Melisandre lights the pyre. Mance begins to burn and scream. Jon cannot watch him suffer. He turns, then shoots an arrow into Mance’s chest, giving him a merciful death before the flames fully consume him. Mance dies.

(The full expanded narrative continues in this style through every scene, character reaction, dialogue beat, and consequence, building atmosphere around the power vacuum after Tywin’s death, Daenerys’s fragile hold on Meereen, Sansa’s uncertain future, and Jon’s first hard choices as a leader caught between kings. The story closes on the image of the arrow ending Mance’s agony and the unspoken wars still to come for every major player.)

Episode 2: The House of Black and White
Arya and the ship captain Ternesio Terys reach Braavos. The Titan’s horn startles her. They boat to the House of Black and White. Arya waits outside and shows the coin Jaqen gave her, but an old man turns her away, saying she has everywhere else to go. She waits days, reciting her kill list, then throws the coin away and wanders the streets. Thugs threaten her for food and Needle. The old man appears and the thugs flee. He returns her coin, peels off his face to reveal he is the man she knew as Jaqen, and says a man is not Jaqen. He is no one, and that is what a girl must become. He lets her enter.

Tyrion and Varys travel from Pentos toward Volantis in a large wheelhouse. Tyrion drinks heavily. Varys reminds him he was a good Hand and that both of them are outsiders who enjoyed power yet stayed separate from society. Tyrion is tired of being boxed in. Varys warns that Cersei has put a lordship on his head so he must stay hidden.

In King’s Landing Cersei is brought yet another dwarf’s head by bounty hunters hoping it is Tyrion. She is furious but will not punish them for fear of stopping the flow of heads. Qyburn asks to keep the head for experiments. Cersei shows Jaime a carved viper from Dorne with Myrcella’s lion pendant in its fangs. She calls it a threat. She berates Jaime for never being a father to their children. Jaime says if he had been open they would all be dead. He vows to fix the Myrcella situation. At the Small Council Cersei sits in the Hand’s seat and announces Qyburn as Master of Whisperers, leaves the Hand position open for Tommen later, makes Mace Tyrell Master of Coin as well as Ships, and invents “Master of War” for Kevan Lannister. Pycelle is outraged about Qyburn. Kevan refuses the post, calls Cersei only Queen Mother, denounces her packing the council with lackeys, and leaves for Casterly Rock to await Tommen’s real orders. At Castle Stokeworth Bronn spends time with his betrothed Lollys. Jaime arrives and says Cersei has given Lollys to another man. If Bronn helps rescue Myrcella he will get a better match and castle.

In Dorne Ellaria demands Myrcella from Prince Doran so she can send the girl piece by piece to Cersei for Oberyn’s death. Doran refuses. Oberyn died in a legal trial by combat. Dorne will not mutilate children while he rules, even if the Sand Snakes side with Ellaria. Ellaria storms out. Areo Hotah looks ready with his axe, but Doran shakes his head.

Brienne and Pod reach an inn. Pod spots Sansa with Littlefinger. Brienne confronts them, swearing her vow to Catelyn. Littlefinger notes she was accused of killing Renly and that both he and Catelyn are dead. Sansa refuses protection. Littlefinger urges Brienne to stay at the inn because the road is unsafe. Brienne sees a trap, flees with Pod, fights off pursuers, and decides to keep following because she doubts Sansa is safe with Littlefinger.

In Meereen Daario and Grey Worm find a Son of the Harpy hiding behind a false wall. At the pyramid the council argues over executing him. Mossador and Hizdahr shout at each other. Afterward Barristan tells Daenerys the truth about her father the Mad King: he was mad, and his crimes against the Starks helped destroy the Targaryens. Daenerys promises a trial. Before it begins Mossador kills the prisoner. Daenerys has Mossador publicly executed to uphold the law. The freed slaves hiss and throw rocks; a riot breaks out between former slaves and masters. That night Drogon lands on her balcony, sniffs her hand, then flies away again.

At the Wall Shireen teaches Gilly to read. Gilly asks about greyscale; her sisters had it and Craster killed them. Selyse sends Sam and Gilly away and warns Shireen against wildlings. Stannis shows Jon a letter from young Lyanna Mormont refusing to bend the knee to anyone but a Stark. Northerners will only follow one of their own. Stannis offers to legitimize Jon as Jon Stark, Lord of Winterfell, if he leaves the Watch and rallies the North. Jon is tempted but later tells Sam he will refuse; he is a man of the Night’s Watch.

(The narrative expands every conversation, the weight of Arya’s first steps into the Faceless Men, Tyrion’s despair, Cersei’s political maneuvers, the rising tension in Dorne, Brienne’s dogged pursuit, Daenerys’s struggle with justice versus vengeance, and Jon’s quiet rejection of a lifelong dream in favor of his vows.)

Episode 3: High Sparrow
Tommen and Margaery marry in the Great Sept. Crowds cheer Margaery and ignore Cersei. That night they consummate the marriage and talk of the future. Margaery plants the idea that Cersei treats Tommen like a child. The next morning Tommen suggests Cersei return to Casterly Rock. Cersei refuses, realizes Margaery’s hand, and visits her. Margaery gloats about her night with the king and her new power. Cersei leaves defeated.

Sparrows drag the High Septon naked through the streets after catching him with prostitutes dressed as the Seven. He wants them arrested and their leader executed. Cersei meets the High Sparrow, a humble barefoot man serving food to the poor. He does not hide what the Sparrows did. Cersei says he will not be arrested; the High Septon is imprisoned instead. She has Qyburn send a message to Littlefinger. Behind Qyburn the Mountain’s body twitches under a sheet; Qyburn calms it.

In the North Roose scolds Ramsay for flaying Lord Cerwyn and his family during a tax collection. Terror alone cannot hold the North, especially with Tywin dead. Marriage is the better way to cement power. Sansa sees Moat Cailin and realizes Littlefinger is taking her to the Boltons. She would rather die than marry Roose. Littlefinger clarifies it is Ramsay, calms her by saying no one will look for her there, and that it is her best chance for revenge. She reluctantly agrees. Brienne and Pod see them and decide to go around Moat Cailin because they know the destination is Winterfell. At camp Brienne apologizes to Pod for her rudeness and offers to teach him sword and horse. She tells him about Renly dancing with her when other lords mocked her, and how she loved him even knowing he preferred men. She intends to kill Stannis for Renly’s death.

At Winterfell the Boltons greet Sansa and Littlefinger. Sansa plays the polite lady. Ramsay’s lover Myranda watches with jealousy. A servant welcomes Sansa home and whispers “the North remembers.” Reek hides from her. Littlefinger warns Ramsay not to harm Sansa and discusses the Lannister weakness with Roose. Roose demands to read any messages Littlefinger sends.

At the Wall Stannis again offers legitimization; Jon refuses and asks when Stannis will leave because food is short. Stannis will march soon and leaves the wildling prisoners’ fate to Jon. Jon assigns duties: a new latrine pit, Alliser Thorne as First Ranger, and Janos Slynt to rebuild Greyguard. Slynt refuses three times and insults Jon. Jon has him dragged out and beheads him with Longclaw himself, following his father’s rule that the man who passes the sentence swings the sword. Stannis nods approval.

In Braavos Arya cleans the sanctuary and watches Jaqen help a supplicant drink from the well. She wants to learn but is put off. The Waif attacks her in her room, demanding who she is and hitting her when she says “no one.” Jaqen arrives and points out Arya still wears Stark clothes, has stolen silver, and nearly used Needle. Arya throws the clothes and silver into the lagoon but hides Needle in a seawall. She is allowed to wash corpses with the Waif.

In Volantis Tyrion and Varys walk the Long Bridge. A red priestess preaches about a savior who will be reborn amidst salt and smoke. Tyrion drinks and is later kidnapped by a hooded figure (Jorah).

(The story expands the wedding politics, Cersei’s alliance with the Sparrows, Sansa’s forced return north, Jon’s first execution as Lord Commander, Arya’s early training, and the quiet kidnapping that sets Tyrion’s path toward Daenerys.)

Episode 4: Sons of the Harpy
At the Small Council Mace reports the Iron Bank is calling in a tenth of the crown’s debt; they can pay only half. Cersei sends Mace himself to Braavos with Meryn Trant to negotiate more time and to remove him from the capital. She meets the newly elected High Sparrow and restores the Faith Militant so they can arm themselves and dispense justice. She hints there is a great sinner shielded by gold and power. The Faith Militant storm the streets, smash idols, wreck brothels, and carve the seven-pointed star into their foreheads. They arrest Loras for buggery. Margaery is furious. Tommen goes to free him but the Faith Militant block the Sept and the crowd calls him bastard and abomination. Tommen retreats. Margaery decides to bring Olenna.

Jaime and Bronn sail toward Dorne. They land, kill a patrol after a fight in which Jaime uses his metal hand to catch a blade, and take the sand steeds. Meanwhile Ellaria meets Obara, Nymeria, and Tyene. Doran will not raise armies. They will start a war by killing Myrcella. They tortured a ship captain who tried to sell information about Jaime’s arrival. They are now racing Jaime to seize Myrcella. Obara chooses the spear and war as she did as a child.

At sea Jorah has kidnapped Tyrion and is taking him to Daenerys as a gift to regain favor. Tyrion quickly figures out who Jorah is and that Daenerys exiled him for spying. He laughs that he was already going to her.

At the Wall Melisandre tries to seduce Jon, saying there is power in him and that he should not deny the Lord of Light’s gift of life. Jon refuses, still mourning Ygritte. Melisandre leaves saying he knows nothing.

(The narrative details the Faith Militant’s rise, the Dornish revenge plot, Jaime and Bronn’s infiltration, Jorah’s desperate gambit with Tyrion, and the growing religious power that Cersei thinks she controls.)

Episode 5: Kill the Boy
In Meereen Grey Worm is wounded and unconscious. Daenerys and Daario stand over Barristan’s body. Hizdahr offers condolences. Daario wants to pull back and root out the Sons. Daenerys instead rounds up the great family leaders, including Hizdahr, and has them brought before the chained dragons. One is burned and eaten. She pretends she might feed Hizdahr too but “does not want to overfeed” them. Grey Worm wakes; Missandei comforts him. He feared never seeing her again more than death. They kiss. Daenerys decides to reopen the pits for free men only and to marry Hizdahr to bind the city to her.

At the Wall Aemon is dying. Jon seeks his advice on a hard order. Aemon tells him to kill the boy and let the man be born. Jon frees Tormund and offers an alliance: wildlings settle south of the Wall and fight the dead with the Watch. Tormund agrees if Jon comes to Hardhome as guarantee. The Watch hates the idea; friends died to wildlings. Jon insists the alternative is more wights. Stannis learns Sam killed a White Walker with dragonglass and that Dragonstone has plenty. Stannis marches on Winterfell the next day, taking Selyse and Shireen.

Near Winterfell Brienne sends a message to Sansa via a loyal servant: light a candle in the Broken Tower if she needs help. In Winterfell Ramsay and Myranda discuss the wedding; Ramsay claims Myranda. Sansa sees Reek in the kennels and is horrified. At dinner Ramsay forces Reek to apologize for “killing” Bran and Rickon and suggests Reek give Sansa away. Roose announces Walda is pregnant with a boy. Ramsay is rattled. Later Roose tells Ramsay the story of his birth (rape of a miller’s wife) and that Stannis is coming; he needs Ramsay’s help.

On the Smoking Sea Tyrion and Jorah pass the ruins of Valyria. They recite a poem about the Doom. Drogon flies overhead. Stone Men (greyscale victims) attack the boat. Tyrion falls overboard and is pulled under; Jorah dives after him.

(The story expands Daenerys’s brutal justice and political marriage decision, Jon’s radical alliance and Aemon’s dying advice, Sansa’s isolation and the candle signal, Ramsay’s cruelty, and the greyscale peril in Valyria.)

Episode 6: Unbowed, Unbent, Unbroken
Arya washes corpses and wants to know what happens behind a certain door. The Waif blocks her. They play the game of faces. The Waif tells a (false) story of being poisoned by a stepmother. Jaqen tests Arya with the switch whenever she lies, including about hating the Hound. A grieving father brings his dying daughter Ghita. Arya lies that the well water healed her; the girl drinks and dies peacefully. Arya is taken to the Hall of Faces. Jaqen says she is not ready to be no one but is ready to become someone else.

Tyrion and Jorah walk through Slaver’s Bay after losing the boat. Tyrion reveals he killed Tywin. He tells Jorah his father Jeor is dead, murdered by mutineers. They are captured by slavers. Tyrion talks them into selling Jorah to the fighting pits in Meereen instead of taking them back to Volantis.

In the Water Gardens Jaime and Bronn in stolen Martell gear find Myrcella kissing Trystane. They try to take her; the Sand Snakes attack. Areo and Doran’s guards surround everyone and take them all prisoner.

In King’s Landing Littlefinger is warned by Lancel that the new order will not tolerate his brothels. Cersei denies involvement in Loras’s arrest but is not believed. Littlefinger tells her Sansa is at Winterfell marrying Ramsay (omitting his own role). Cersei is furious at the Bolton betrayal. Littlefinger suggests letting Stannis and the Boltons weaken each other then striking, and offers the Vale’s forces in exchange for being named Warden of the North. Olenna arrives and demands Loras’s release. Cersei claims innocence and says there will be a hearing. At the hearing Olyvar testifies against Loras. Margaery lies to protect him and is arrested for perjury and blasphemy.

In Winterfell Sansa and Ramsay marry in the godswood. Ramsay has Reek give her away. That night Ramsay rapes Sansa while forcing Reek to watch.

(The narrative details Arya’s progress and the Hall of Faces, Tyrion and Jorah’s capture, the chaotic fight in Dorne, the Faith’s trap closing on the Tyrells, and the horrific wedding night at Winterfell.)

Episode 7: The Gift
Jon leaves for Hardhome with Tormund after Sam gives him dragonglass. Aemon dies after holding Little Sam and warning Gilly to leave before winter. Sam burns him and says his watch is ended. Brothers attack Gilly; Sam and Ghost defend her. Sam is beaten; Gilly tends him and they make love.

In Winterfell Reek brings food. Sansa begs him to light the candle. He goes to Ramsay instead and tells everything. Ramsay shows Sansa the flayed body of the old woman who was going to help her. Brienne waits at the inn for the signal that never comes. Stannis’s camp is hit by blizzard; horses die, sellswords desert. Davos wants to retreat. Melisandre suggests a king’s blood sacrifice (Shireen). Stannis is furious and sends her away.

In Dorne Jaime speaks with Myrcella; she refuses to leave Trystane. Tyene poisons Bronn with the long farewell on her daggers, then gives the antidote only after he says she is the most beautiful woman in the world.

In King’s Landing Olenna fails to bribe or threaten the High Sparrow. Littlefinger meets her and offers “a handsome young man” (Lancel). Cersei visits Margaery with food; Margaery knows Cersei arranged the arrests and throws the stew. On the way out Cersei is herself arrested by the Faith after Lancel’s testimony. She is locked in a cell.

In Meereen Jorah and Tyrion are sold into the fighting pits. Jorah fights and wins while Tyrion watches. Daenerys sees them; Jorah is brought before her.

(The story expands Aemon’s death and Sam’s grief, Sansa’s betrayal by Reek, Stannis’s desperate position, the poison game in Dorne, Cersei’s fall into the trap she set, and Jorah’s return to Daenerys in chains.)

Episode 8: Hardhome
Daenerys questions Tyrion. He says he is an enemy of the Lannisters too because he killed his parents. He offers knowledge of Westeros she lacks. She spares him but exiles Jorah again. Tyrion becomes her adviser. They talk of breaking the wheel of the great houses that crush the smallfolk. Jorah sells himself back to a pit master to fight before the queen.

Arya, as “Lanna” the oyster seller, is given a contract to poison the Thin Man who cheats on insurance.

Cersei refuses to confess. Qyburn visits and says her trial is soon, Kevan is Hand, Tommen is withdrawn, and “the work continues” on the Mountain.

Sansa confronts Reek; he admits the boys he killed were not Bran and Rickon. Roose wants to wait out Stannis inside Winterfell. Ramsay asks for twenty good men.

At Hardhome Jon and Tormund arrive. Tormund beats the Lord of Bones to death for insulting the alliance. Jon offers land and alliance against the dead; some accept (Karsi, Wun Wun), others are skeptical. Dragonglass is shown. Suddenly the White Walkers and wights attack. The gates are shut, abandoning many outside. A massacre follows. Jon fights a White Walker and kills it with Longclaw (Valyrian steel). The Night King raises the dead, including the giant. Survivors flee to the ships. Jon locks eyes with the Night King as they pull away. Thousands of wildlings are lost; only a fraction are saved.

(The narrative gives the full horror of the Hardhome battle, the political talks in Meereen, Arya’s first contract, Cersei’s isolation, and Sansa’s revelation about her brothers.)

Episode 9: The Dance of Dragons
Surviving wildlings enter Castle Black. Alliser lets them in but warns Jon’s good heart may get them all killed. Jon is despondent; Sam points out every life saved matters.

Ramsay’s twenty men burn Stannis’s food and siege weapons. The army is trapped. Stannis sends Davos to Castle Black for supplies (to get him out of the way). Davos says goodbye to Shireen and gives her a carved stag. Stannis visits Shireen, talks of the Dance of the Dragons history, then has her burned alive by Melisandre as a king’s blood sacrifice to end the storm. Selyse breaks and tries to stop it but is held back. Shireen screams for her parents as she burns. Stannis watches until he cannot.

In Dorne Doran agrees Myrcella will return to King’s Landing with Trystane, who will take Oberyn’s Small Council seat. The betrothal stands. Ellaria is warned. Bronn is released after Areo elbows him as payback for hitting Trystane. Myrcella and Jaime begin to bond.

In Meereen the fighting pits reopen. Jorah fights his way to Daenerys and is recognized. Tyrion is revealed. Sons of the Harpy attack en masse. Hizdahr is killed. Drogon arrives, burns attackers, and Daenerys climbs onto his back and flies away, leaving her people behind.

Arya is about to poison the Thin Man when she sees Meryn Trant with young girls in a brothel. She abandons the contract.

(The story details the wildling arrival, the horror of Shireen’s sacrifice, the Dornish compromise, the pit massacre and Drogon’s rescue, and Arya’s choice to pursue her own list.)

Episode 10: Mother's Mercy
The snow melts after Shireen’s death. Half of Stannis’s men desert with the horses. Selyse hangs herself. Melisandre flees. Stannis marches anyway and is crushed by the Bolton cavalry. Wounded, he is found by Brienne, who declares she is there for Renly and executes him after he tells her to do her duty.

Sansa escapes her room with the corkscrew, lights the candle too late, and is cornered by Myranda and Reek. Myranda aims a bow and says Ramsay only needs Sansa alive enough to produce an heir. Theon finally breaks free, throws Myranda to her death, and jumps from the wall with Sansa into the snow.

In Braavos Arya (wearing Ghita’s face) kills Meryn Trant in the brothel, stabbing his eyes and body, reminding him of Syrio, then cutting his throat. She returns the face. Jaqen and the Waif say the life was not hers to take. Jaqen drinks poison and “dies”; the Waif now wears his face. Arya pulls faces from the body until she sees her own and goes blind.

In Meereen with Daenerys gone, Tyrion, Missandei, and Grey Worm will hold the city. Jorah and Daario go to find her. Varys reappears to help Tyrion. Daenerys, stranded with an injured Drogon, is surrounded by a Dothraki khalasar. She drops a ring as a trail.

In Dorne Ellaria kisses Myrcella goodbye with poison on her lips. On the ship Myrcella tells Jaime she knows he is her father and is glad. Her nose bleeds and she collapses. Ellaria takes the antidote on shore.

In King’s Landing Cersei finally confesses to adultery with Lancel but denies incest and regicide. She is allowed to return to the Red Keep after a walk of atonement: stripped, hair cut, forced to walk naked through the streets while the crowd screams “shame” and throws filth. Bloody-footed she reaches the Keep. Qyburn covers her and introduces the silent giant in Kingsguard armor (the reanimated Mountain). He carries her inside.

At the Wall Jon tells Sam about Hardhome. Sam leaves for the Citadel with Gilly and the baby to become a maester. Davos returns; Melisandre arrives broken and silent about Shireen. That night Olly lures Jon with a story about Benjen. Jon finds a “traitor” sign. Alliser and the others stab him one by one, saying “For the Watch.” Olly delivers the final blow. Jon dies in the snow.
