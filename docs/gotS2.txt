Episode 1: The North Remembers

The Night's Watch ranging party under Lord Commander Jeor Mormont reaches Craster's Keep after passing empty wildling villages. Jon Snow, Samwell Tarly, Grenn, and Eddison Tollett find Craster repulsive. He keeps many wives who are also his daughters and has no sons. Craster claims he has not seen the missing Benjen Stark and says the wildlings are gathering under Mance Rayder, the King-Beyond-the-Wall. Jon challenges Craster's crude insults toward Mormont. Mormont reprimands Jon, explaining that Craster provides needed shelter and information in the harsh lands north of the Wall despite his disgusting ways. Mormont tells Jon that to lead one day he must first learn to follow. The keep is a rough, filthy place filled with Craster's fear and control over his household. The rangers rest uneasily there as the cold winds blow and the threat of what lies further north hangs over them.

In King's Landing, King Joffrey Baratheon celebrates his nameday with a tournament on the walls of the Red Keep. Ser Dontos Hollard arrives late and drunk. Joffrey orders his men to funnel an entire barrel of wine down Dontos's throat until he dies. Sansa Stark steps in, saying it is bad luck to kill on one's nameday. Sandor Clegane supports her. Joffrey makes Dontos his new court fool instead. Tyrion Lannister arrives in the city. Joffrey is disdainful, but Tommen and Myrcella are happy to see their uncle alive. The Small Council meets and learns from the Conclave that the long summer has ended. Cersei orders the city gates closed against war refugees. Tyrion presents Tywin's letter naming him acting Hand of the King. Cersei is furious and suspicious. Tyrion criticizes her failure to control Joffrey and the disastrous decisions, especially Ned Stark's execution, which started the war they are losing. He is shocked Arya escaped, noting how much better their position would be with three Stark hostages. Tyrion hides Shae in the Tower of the Hand against his father's orders. Shae is excited by the big city.

Cersei tasks Petyr Baelish with finding Arya. She mocks his old love for Catelyn. Baelish hints at her relationship with Jaime, saying knowledge is power. Cersei has her guards seize him and threaten to cut his throat, then stops them, saying power is power. Joffrey redecorates the throne room to make it more intimidating like the Targaryen days. He refuses to ask Tywin for help finding Arya. He blames Tywin for war losses and Jaime's capture. Hearing rumors about his parentage, Joffrey confronts Cersei. She dismisses it as enemy gossip. He asks about Robert's bastards and insults her by saying Robert grew tired of her. She slaps him. He warns her never to hit him again or he will have her killed. Janos Slynt leads a massacre of Robert's bastard children across the city. Baby Barra is killed in Littlefinger's brothel in front of her mother and Ros. Tobho Mott is questioned and reveals Gendry left with the Night's Watch recruits, identifiable by his bull's head helmet. Gendry and Arya travel north on the Kingsroad with Yoren's group.

At Winterfell, Bran deals with the petty complaints of vassals unhappy that Robb called the banners and took the young men south. Maester Luwin guides him in lordly duties. Bran dreams he is his direwolf Summer walking in the godswood. Looking in the pool he sees Summer's face instead of his own and wakes. He has Osha and Hodor take him to the pool to confirm his own reflection. They talk about the red comet. Osha believes it means dragons have returned to the world.

On Dragonstone, Stannis Baratheon lets Melisandre burn the statues of the Seven as offerings to the Lord of Light. Maester Cressen is outraged at the insult to the old gods and fears Melisandre is leading Stannis into a hopeless war. Melisandre proclaims Stannis the champion of the Lord of Light as he draws a burning sword from a statue. Cressen asks Davos for help, but Davos says the timing is wrong. Stannis has Ned Stark's letter naming him Robert's true heir. He sends copies across the realm declaring Joffrey a bastard born of incest between Cersei and Jaime and claiming the throne. He rejects Davos's suggestion of alliance with Renly or Robb. Renly has the bigger army but Stannis sees him as a usurper; Robb wants Northern independence which Stannis views as theft of half his kingdom. Cressen pretends to apologize and poisons a cup of wine, hoping Melisandre will share it. Davos sees the poison and tries to stop him. Cressen drinks first then offers it to her. Melisandre drinks unharmed while Cressen dies.

In the Riverlands, Robb receives Stannis's letter. He questions captive Jaime about crippling Bran. Jaime reveals nothing and mocks Robb for keeping him close instead of with a bannerman, showing lack of trust. Robb says he trusts his men but knows the Lannisters would go to any lengths to free Jaime. Grey Wind, nearly grown, menaces Jaime. Robb sends Alton Lannister to King's Landing with peace terms: release of his sisters, return of Ned's bones and those who served him, and recognition of the North as an independent kingdom. Theon asks to go to Pyke to seek alliance with Balon, saying they need ships to attack King's Landing. Catelyn warns against trusting Balon after his past rebellion. She wants to trade Jaime for the girls and return to her younger sons, but Robb sends her to seek alliance with Renly instead and sends Rodrik Cassel to Winterfell.

In the Red Waste, Daenerys leads her small remaining khalasar east to avoid rival khals who would kill her for the dragons. The dragons struggle to eat the little meat available. Her people starve. Her beloved white mare, Drogo's first gift, dies. She stops the march and sends three bloodriders in different directions to scout for food, water, and refuge. The endless red desert stretches around them under a merciless sun, with no end in sight and enemies closing in.

Episode 2: The Night Lands

In the Riverlands, Arya continues posing as a boy named Arry while traveling with Yoren and the Night's Watch recruits. She urinates in private to hide her sex. She is drawn to the caged prisoners Jaqen H'ghar, Rorge, and Biter. Jaqen asks for water and introduces himself. Gold Cloaks approach seeking Gendry, one of Robert's bastards. Arya hides, thinking they want her. Yoren intimidates them into leaving empty-handed, though they promise to return with more men. Arya asks Gendry why he is wanted. He does not know but recalls being questioned by Jon Arryn and Ned Stark. He has seen through her disguise. Arya reveals she is Arya Stark and makes him swear secrecy.

In King's Landing, Varys finds Shae living with Tyrion in the Tower of the Hand and implies threats about revealing her. Tyrion warns Varys not to underestimate him. Alton delivers Robb's peace terms. Cersei rejects them. Pycelle shares a Night's Watch message requesting men and warning of wights. Only Tyrion takes it seriously. Ros struggles after witnessing Barra's murder. Littlefinger threatens her with a story of selling an unhappy girl to a depraved man to recover his investment and orders her to return happy after a night off. Tyrion dines with Janos Slynt and questions him about the bastard massacre. Janos admits involvement but will not name who ordered it. Tyrion arrests him, exiles him to the Night's Watch, and makes Bronn commander of the City Watch. Cersei confronts Tyrion. He warns she is losing the people's support. She claims not to care. He realizes Joffrey ordered the killings. He jokes about her and Jaime. She says he has always been funny, then ends by saying his finest joke was killing their mother in childbirth.

On Dragonstone, Davos recruits the Lysene sellsail Salladhor Saan and his thirty ships for Stannis. Matthos urges Davos to learn to read the Lord of Light texts, but Davos puts his faith in Stannis alone. Melisandre seduces Stannis with the promise of a son. He hesitates because of his wife Selyse but gives in.

On the Iron Islands, Theon arrives by merchant ship after sleeping with the captain's daughter. He expects a warm welcome but no one meets him at Lordsport. A woman offers him a ride to Pyke and he tries to seduce her. Balon questions Theon's loyalty after nine years as a Stark ward. Theon delivers Robb's letter offering alliance against the Lannisters and a crown for Balon. The guide enters and Balon greets her warmly—she is Theon's sister Yara. Balon rejects the offer, comparing Theon unfavorably to Yara. He will pay the iron price and take what he wants by force. No one gives him a crown. When Theon says he cannot beat the Lannisters, Balon replies he has no intention of fighting them.

In the Red Waste, Daenerys's khalasar waits exhausted for the bloodriders, water nearly gone. Rakharo's horse returns riderless. Jorah finds his head and severed braid in the saddlebag. A rival khalasar killed him. Irri is devastated, believing the mutilation bars him from the Night Lands. Daenerys promises a funeral.

At Craster's Keep, Sam rescues Gilly from Ghost when the direwolf bothers her for food. She confides she wants to flee because she fears what Craster will do if her unborn child is a boy. She begs Jon to take her, but will not say why. Jon says it is impossible as they go deeper into wildling lands. Sam and Jon argue about it. Jon later sees Craster take a baby into the woods at night. He follows and watches Craster leave the child for the White Walkers. Before Jon can act, Craster knocks him out.

Episode 3: What Is Dead May Never Die

Craster drags the beaten Jon back to the keep after catching him spying on the baby sacrifice. He orders the Night's Watch out, blaming Jon. Mormont questions Jon and reveals he has long known Craster sacrifices his sons. Craster is too valuable for information and shelter to risk offending. Mormont is disgusted by the incest and sacrifices but will not interfere, forbidding Jon from doing so. Craster has helped many, including Benjen. Jon says he saw something take the child. Mormont says Jon will see it again. Sam gives Gilly a thimble that belonged to his mother and promises to return for her.

At Winterfell, Bran dreams of seeing through Summer's eyes, following Hodor into his own bedroom and looking down on himself sleeping. He tells Luwin, who dismisses the dreams as unimportant.

In the Stormlands, Catelyn arrives at Renly's camp during a tournament. She watches Brienne of Tarth defeat Loras Tyrell and win a place in Renly's Kingsguard. Renly introduces his new wife Margaery Tyrell and shows off his 100,000-strong army. Catelyn warns him to take the war seriously. Renly tries to have sex with Loras but is rebuffed. Loras reminds him of the need for an heir and fetches Margaery. She fails to seduce Renly but is pragmatic about his preferences and suggests including her brother, stressing the importance of producing an heir.

On the Iron Islands, Theon sits in Balon's war council. Balon plans to attack the lightly defended North while its armies fight the Lannisters. Theon is assigned one ship to raid Stony Shore fishing villages while Yara commands thirty ships to take Deepwood Motte. Theon begs reconsideration, saying attacking the North risks total destruction if they lose, while allying with the Starks could win Balon the Iron Islands crown plus Casterly Rock. Balon refuses and hits him for his Stark loyalty. Theon considers warning Robb but instead reaffirms family loyalty by being baptized by a Drowned Man, drowning and rising again.

In King's Landing, Shae complains of confinement. Tyrion works with Varys to find her a job for more freedom. Cersei hosts an awkward dinner with her children and captive Sansa. Sansa maintains her loyal façade. Shae arrives as Sansa's new handmaiden, giving Sansa someone to confide in. Tyrion tests Small Council loyalty by feeding each a different marriage plan for Myrcella. Cersei confronts him about the plan given to Pycelle, exposing Pycelle as her spy. Tyrion insists on the Dornish marriage alliance over Cersei's protests and has Bronn arrest Pycelle. Littlefinger is angry at being used. Tyrion offers him the chance to act as envoy to Renly and Catelyn.

In the Riverlands, Arya suffers insomnia. Yoren advises her to focus on retribution for her painful memories. Ser Amory Lorch arrives with Lannister men and Gold Cloaks demanding Gendry. Yoren refuses and fights bravely but dies. Arya frees Jaqen from a burning prisoner wagon. Lommy picks up Gendry's helmet and is wounded. Polliver takes Needle from Arya. Gendry and Hot Pie are captured. Lommy begs to be carried; Polliver stabs him in the throat with Needle. Arya claims Lommy was Gendry because of the helmet. The prisoners are taken to Harrenhal.

Episode 4: Garden of Bones

Robb's Northern army launches a surprise night assault on a Lannister camp at Oxcross. Grey Wind causes heavy damage. It is a crushing victory, another Whispering Wood as Robb promised Cersei. The next day Silent Sisters tend the wounded. Roose Bolton argues for killing all Lannister prisoners because supplies are short and prisoners slow them down. Robb refuses, insisting on honorable conduct under the laws of war so the Lannisters have no excuse to torture Sansa and Arya. Robb sees Talisa of Volantis treating a wounded Lannister soldier whose leg must be amputated. He helps hold the man down. Talisa criticizes the war. Robb wants only revenge on Joffrey for Ned's death and independence for the North afterward; he does not care who takes the Iron Throne. She points out he has not hurt the real killers, Joffrey and Tywin, and has slaughtered unwilling boy soldiers instead of Tywin's main force.

Arya, Gendry, and Hot Pie arrive at Harrenhal with other prisoners under Amory Lorch. The vast ruined castle still bears the scars of dragonfire from Aegon's Conquest. Gregor Clegane, the Mountain, commands. The stench of corpses is overwhelming. The Tickler and others sadistically torture prisoners—tying a rat in a bucket to a man's belly and heating it so the rat eats through him—seeking hidden valuables or information on the Brotherhood Without Banners. Gendry is about to be tortured when Tywin arrives with the main Lannister host retreating after Whispering Wood. Tywin halts the tortures as inefficient and puts the prisoners to work. He immediately notices "Arry" is a girl and is impressed by her intelligence when she explains dressing as a boy was safer. He makes her his cupbearer.

In King's Landing, Joffrey is furious at Robb's victory. He has Sansa brought before the court in the redecorated throne room. Lancel claims Robb won by trickery and sorcery. Joffrey threatens Sansa with a crossbow, considering killing her as a message. She refuses to show fear and pledges loyalty. He has Meryn Trant beat and strip her, ordering her face left pretty. The court watches in horror; no one intervenes. Sandor is disgusted. Tyrion storms in, stops it, and has Sandor cover her with his cloak. He berates Joffrey for shaming his future queen and hostage, calling him a halfwit and comparing him to the Mad King. When Meryn speaks, Tyrion orders Bronn to kill him if he opens his mouth again. Tyrion comforts Sansa. She still claims love and duty for Joffrey. Tyrion respects her survival skills. Bronn suggests giving Joffrey prostitutes to release frustration. Tyrion sends Ros and Daisy. Joffrey forces Ros at crossbow-point to beat Daisy unconscious and dump her in Tyrion's chambers as a warning. Late at night Lancel delivers Cersei's order to free Pycelle. Tyrion traps him into admitting he comes from Cersei's bed smelling of her perfume. Tyrion blackmails Lancel into spying on Cersei, threatening to tell Joffrey that Lancel killed Robert and sleeps with his mother.

In the Stormlands, Littlefinger arrives at Renly's camp as Joffrey's envoy. He hints he can help from inside if Renly marches on the capital. He tries to intimidate Margaery about Renly's preferences, but she already knows and does not care; she entered the marriage for the Tyrell-Renly alliance. He meets Catelyn, who is still furious at his betrayal of Ned. He tries to rekindle romance; she draws a dagger. He presents Tyrion's offer of a prisoner exchange—Jaime for Sansa and Arya (lying that they have Arya)—and as good faith brings Ned's bones. Catelyn grieves then hardens with resolve. Stannis arrives with Melisandre and Davos to demand Renly yield. Renly refuses, citing his huge army. Catelyn criticizes both brothers for childishness. Stannis offers Renly his old Small Council seat and to name him heir if he yields. Renly declines. Melisandre warns Renly the night is dark and full of terrors. Later Davos rows pregnant Melisandre through caves near Renly's camp. She births a shadow creature as Davos watches in horror.

In Qarth, Kovarro returns with news of the city. The desert around it is called the Garden of Bones because those turned away die of thirst. Daenerys reaches the gates. The Thirteen demand to see the dragons first. She insists her people be fed and sheltered. They refuse and prepare to abandon her. She swears fire and blood vengeance. Xaro Xhoan Daxos vouchsafes for her with a blood oath. They are allowed inside.

Episode 5: The Ghost of Harrenhal

Renly is assassinated in his tent by the shadow baby Melisandre birthed with Stannis, just after agreeing to ally with Robb. Brienne is blamed and flees with Catelyn. Brienne swears fealty to Catelyn. Renly's army collapses; the Tyrells return to Highgarden while others join Stannis. Littlefinger goes with the Tyrells, seeing opportunity. Davos challenges Stannis on using Melisandre's magic and convinces him to leave her behind for the attack on King's Landing to avoid rumors. Stannis puts Davos in command of the fleet. Alone, Stannis briefly grieves his brother.

Tyrion brings news of Renly's death to Cersei. She is pleased, but he predicts Stannis's attack. She keeps defense plans secret. Through Lancel, Tyrion learns she has been meeting the Alchemists' Guild. He visits Wisdom Hallyne and discovers thousands of jars of wildfire stockpiled as catapult ammunition. Bronn warns it could burn the city itself if mishandled. Tyrion takes control of the wildfire from Cersei. He passes a street protest blaming him for the city's ills.

Theon takes command of his ship but finds the crew insubordinate. Dagmer says he must prove himself. Theon plans a feint on Torrhen's Square to draw Winterfell's remaining forces away. It works; Rodrik leads 200 men to relieve it.

Bran gives good counsel but is troubled by dreams of a three-eyed raven. Osha will not interpret. He also dreams of the sea flooding Winterfell and drowning people including Rodrik.

At Harrenhal, Tywin holds a long war council on strategy against Robb. Arya serves as cupbearer. Tywin realizes she is Northern and questions her without knowing her identity. She avoids revealing herself. Fetching water she meets Jaqen, now a Lannister man-at-arms. Because she saved him and the other two prisoners, he owes her three deaths. She names the Tickler. Soon the Tickler is found dead with his neck twisted completely around. Jaqen signals to Arya from above.

The Night's Watch reaches the Fist of the First Men and meets scout Qhorin. He warns their tactics must change against Mance Rayder's army and suggests a small party to neutralize lookouts in the Skirling Pass. Jon joins Qhorin's group. Sam becomes Mormont's steward.

In Qarth, Daenerys has taught her dragons to breathe fire on command and they eat meat independently. Xaro hosts a gathering. She is disturbed by warlock Pyat Pree's tricks and his invitation to the House of the Undying. Quaithe warns Jorah that Daenerys needs protection from those who lust after her dragons. Xaro offers to fund her Westeros invasion for her hand in marriage. Jorah advises against accepting and reveals his feelings for her, which she does not return. He says she can win her throne with just one ship if she reaches Westeros.

Episode 6: The Old Gods and the New

At Winterfell, Luwin frantically sends a raven. Theon and Ironborn raiders take the undefended castle after the Torrhen's Square feint. Theon uses grappling claws to infiltrate. He forces Bran to surrender to save the people. Rodrik is captured returning from Torrhen's Square. He spits on Theon. Dagmer insists Theon must kill him or lose face. Theon botches the beheading with multiple hacks and a kick, horrifying everyone. That night Osha sleeps with Theon then sneaks out, frees Bran, Rickon, Hodor, and the direwolves, kills a guard, and escapes.

In the Westerlands, Robb talks with Talisa, who has followed the army. He introduces her to Catelyn and Brienne, returned from the Stormlands. Roose brings Luwin's raven news of Winterfell's fall. Robb wants to march north immediately. Roose advises leaving it to his bastard Ramsay Snow at the Dreadfort with the remaining Northern forces. Retreating the main army would lose all gains. Robb agrees but wants Theon taken alive so he can ask "why" before executing him.

At Harrenhal, Tywin berates Amory Lorch for delivering a letter to enemies because he cannot read. He discovers Arya can read. Littlefinger arrives and discusses Renly's death, Stannis gaining Stormlands houses, and the Tyrells staying neutral at Highgarden. They discuss a possible Lannister-Tyrell alliance. Tywin questions Arya further. She steals a letter about Robb while he is distracted. Amory sees her with it and grows suspicious. She runs to Jaqen and names Amory as the second death, urgently. Amory dies of a poisoned dart just as he reaches Tywin.

In King's Landing, the court sees Myrcella off to Dorne for the marriage alliance with Trystane Martell. Cersei is heartbroken and blames Tyrion. The streets overflow with starving refugees. On the return, a crowd throws cow dung in Joffrey's face. He orders the entire crowd killed. Riots erupt. The High Septon is torn apart. The royal party barely escapes. Tyrion slaps Joffrey for the stupidity that endangered them all. Sansa is lost in the crowd and nearly raped by a mob before Sandor kills her attackers and carries her to safety. Tyrion thanks him; Sandor says he did not do it for Tyrion.

Beyond the Wall, Qhorin's scouting party fights wildling scouts and captures red-haired Ygritte. Qhorin orders Jon to execute her while the others continue. Jon hesitates; she runs. He catches her but they are separated. Night falls; they must camp together.

In Qarth, Daenerys fails to get ships from the Spice King, who sees her as too risky with no army or Westerosi supporters. Returning to Xaro's, she finds her guards dead, Irri strangled, and the dragons stolen. A hooded figure carries them away in a box.

Episode 7: A Man Without Honor

At Winterfell, Theon wakes to find Osha, Hodor, Bran, Rickon, and the wolves gone. He beats Black Lorren for letting it happen (Lorren notes Theon slept with Osha). Theon hunts with hounds. At a farm they find walnut shells proving the boys were there, but the scent is lost. Theon ends the hunt, saying his mercy is exhausted, and returns. He hangs two burned small children's bodies on the gates. Luwin cries out, believing Bran and Rickon dead.

At Harrenhal, Tywin discusses Amory's assassination with Gregor, convinced it targeted him. Gregor blames the Brotherhood. Tywin orders villages burned in reprisal. Tywin suspects Arya is highborn because she says "my lord" properly instead of "m'lord" and has an educated way of speaking. She claims her mother was a handmaiden who taught her.

In King's Landing, Sansa thanks Sandor for saving her. He says a dog needs no courage against rats and warns that one day his violence will be all that stands between her and Joffrey. Sansa wakes to bloody sheets from her first period. She panics, knowing it means she must marry and bear Joffrey's child. Shae helps hide the sheets, but Sandor finds them. Sansa is brought to Cersei, who tries to comfort her in her way. Cersei describes Robert abandoning her in childbirth while Jaime stayed, and says Sansa should never expect such from Joffrey. Love only your children, Cersei advises, because loving others makes you weak. "You can try" to love Joffrey. Privately Cersei regrets her children with Jaime and wonders if Joffrey's nature is the price of their sins. Tyrion points out Myrcella and Tommen are good. They share a moment of sympathy.

In the Westerlands, Alton returns with Cersei's rejection of peace terms. Robb imprisons him with Jaime. Talisa needs medical supplies; Robb invites her to the Crag to get them. That night Jaime kills Alton to lure guard Torrhen Karstark into the cell, strangles him, and escapes, but is recaptured. Torrhen was Rickard Karstark's son. Rickard demands Jaime's head. Catelyn argues to spare him as a valuable hostage. She confronts Jaime, calling him without honor. Jaime explains his Kingsguard vows required defending the innocent and serving the king, but the Mad King slaughtered innocents. Honor and loyalty are naïve or hypocritical. He says he has only been with Cersei and mocks Ned for fathering a bastard. Catelyn reaches breaking point and asks for Brienne's sword.

Beyond the Wall, Jon travels with captive Ygritte, still separated. They argue about freedom, oaths, and whose lands are whose. Jon says as Ned's son and a Northman he has First Men blood like her. She asks why the Watch fights wildlings when the only difference is living north of the Wall when it was built. She mocks his virginity and offers to teach him. She escapes and leads him into an ambush, saying he should have taken her when he had the chance.

In Qarth, Daenerys desperately seeks her dragons. Jorah returns; she orders him to find them. Xaro claims distress at breaking his protection promise. Quaithe tells Jorah the thief is already with Daenerys and knows Jorah once spied on her. Daenerys pleads with the Thirteen. Pyat Pree confesses to the theft and declares Xaro King of Qarth. Magical doubles of Pyat slit the other Thirteen members' throats. Xaro and Pyat had allied. Pyat urges Daenerys to the House of the Undying for her dragons.

Episode 8: The Prince of Winterfell

Ygritte brings Jon before the Lord of Bones, who wants him executed since he already has Qhorin. Ygritte spares him for his Stark blood. Qhorin plans for Jon to spy among the wildlings. At the Fist, Grenn and Sam find a cache of dragonglass weapons. Jon's absence is noted; Sam refuses to believe he is dead.

In Qarth, Jorah advises Daenerys to abandon the dragons and flee; he has passage to Astapor. She insists on reclaiming them and has him lead her to the House of the Undying.

At Winterfell, Yara arrives with few men, not reinforcements. She advises Theon that Winterfell is too far inland, poorly defended with so few, and the North is rising. She urges him to abandon it and come home. He refuses. Dagmer admits that after killing the orphan boys he also killed the farmer and wife.

Luwin sees Osha enter the crypts and follows. She explains they doubled back using a stream to hide their scent. Luwin urges her not to tell Bran and Rickon about the murdered children. Bran overhears.

Stannis's fleet nears King's Landing. He admires Davos and offers him the Hand of the King position once he takes the throne.

In King's Landing, Tyrion studies history for defense ideas, takes advice from Bronn and Varys, and decides to use the wildfire. Joffrey is full of bravado; Cersei worries and kidnaps Ros, thinking she is Tyrion's lover, as collateral. Tyrion hides his relief that Shae is safe and vows revenge on Cersei.

At Harrenhal, Tywin is torn between defending King's Landing and driving Robb from the Westerlands. Kevan warns Stannis will kill Cersei and Joffrey. Tywin marches west, leaving Arya to serve Gregor. Arya realizes she missed killing Tywin and forces Jaqen to help her escape by naming him as the third death. She will only take the name back if he gets her, Hot Pie, and Gendry out. At midnight they walk through the gates; the guards are dead. They escape.

In the Westerlands, Robb is furious that Catelyn freed Jaime. He calls it betrayal and places her under guard. She sent Brienne to escort Jaime to King's Landing for an exchange for her daughters. Brienne uses a canoe to evade pursuit. Robb plans to turn Theon's men against him with a mercy offer via Ramsay through Roose. Robb seeks comfort with Talisa, breaking his Frey betrothal.

Episode 9: Blackwater

Tyrion prepares the defense of King's Landing against Stannis's assault in the Battle of the Blackwater. He spends the night with Shae, who refuses to leave. Varys gives him a map of the tunnels and says Tyrion alone stands between them and defeat. Bronn drinks with Lannister men and clashes with Sandor.

Stannis plans a naval assault to land near the Mud Gate, unaware of Tyrion's plan. From the battlements Joffrey demands to know why their ships are not engaging. Tyrion fields none. He sends one ship filled with wildfire into Stannis's fleet, leaking the substance. On Tyrion's torch signal from the walls, Bronn fires a flaming arrow from shore, igniting a massive explosion that devastates Stannis's fleet, destroys Davos's ship, and kills Matthos.

Stannis lands men in rowboats farther out to avoid the burning bay, knowing thousands will die. He leads the assault personally and reaches the walls first. Tyrion sends Sandor on a sortie. The Hound is terrified by the fire. Lancel is wounded. Bronn saves the Hound, who retreats after heavy losses, demands wine, and deserts, saying "Fuck the Kingsguard. Fuck the city. Fuck the king."

Cersei shelters the court ladies in Maegor's Holdfast with essence of nightshade from Pycelle and Ilyn Payne ready. She drinks heavily and torments Sansa with descriptions of the rapes that will come if the city falls. She questions Shae. When Lancel reports Stannis at the walls, Cersei orders Joffrey brought back despite morale damage.

Joffrey leaves willingly with Lancel, charging Mandon Moore and Boros Blount to fight in his name. Gold cloak morale breaks. Tyrion rallies the men to fight for their homes and women: "Those are brave men knocking at our door. Let's go kill them!" He leads a tunnel sortie that defeats the battering ram crew. Mandon betrays and slashes Tyrion's face. Podrick kills Mandon from behind. Tyrion, barely conscious, sees the battle seemingly lost.

Lancel returns to warn Cersei they are defeated without Joffrey. She ignores him, hurts his wound, and leaves. Sansa tries to keep the women's morale with the Mother's hymn. Shae tells her to lock herself in her chambers and await Stannis, who would not harm her, unlike Ilyn. The Hound waits in her room and offers to take her north. She refuses, believing she will be safe under Stannis. He tells her the world is full of killers and she must get used to looking at them.

Cersei takes Tommen to the throne room, sits on the Iron Throne with him, tells a story of a young lion who need not fear the wolf or stag, and prepares lethal nightshade doses. Tywin and the Tyrells arrive with cavalry, led by a knight in Renly's armor (Loras). They smash Stannis's forces. Survivors flee. Stannis is dragged away screaming for his men to fight. Tyrion collapses as the tide turns. Tywin enters the throne room announcing victory just as Cersei is about to kill Tommen. Loras reveals himself.

Episode 10: Valar Morghulis

Tyrion recovers in shabby new quarters from Mandon's wound. Pycelle gloats that Tywin has returned and taken over as Hand; Tyrion is no longer acting Hand. Varys reports Bronn is relieved of the gold cloaks and the hill tribes have left with plunder; Tyrion has lost all power. Varys alone thanks him for saving the city. Shae urges him to flee to Pentos with her; he refuses.

Joffrey rewards the victors. Tywin is named Hand and Savior of the City. Littlefinger gets Harrenhal for brokering the Lannister-Tyrell alliance. Loras asks Joffrey to wed Margaery to unite the houses. Cersei and Pycelle convince Joffrey to set aside Sansa given Stark treachery. Joffrey agrees. Sansa is privately overjoyed until Littlefinger notes Joffrey can still torment her, but promises to help her escape for Catelyn's sake. Varys visits Ros (who does not recognize him until learning he is a eunuch), learns she fears Littlefinger, and says he protects those who work for him.

On Dragonstone, Stannis rages at Melisandre for not foreseeing defeat. She says the Lord of Light shows only glimpses and he cannot abandon the war for one loss. He accuses her of lying and begins to strangle her. She says her god is in him; he realizes she means the shadow that killed Renly and releases her. She tells him he will betray men, family, and beliefs but it will be worth it; he is the Prince That Was Promised and will be king. She has him look into a brazier; visions restore his faith.

In the Westerlands, Robb discusses Talisa with Catelyn. She urges him to keep his Frey vow or face consequences, noting she and Ned grew to love each other. Robb ignores her and marries Talisa privately that night. Brienne and Jaime travel east. They find three women hanged by Stark men for sleeping with Lannisters. When the men return, Jaime is recognized. Brienne kills them, revealing her skill. She serves Catelyn, not House Stark.

At Winterfell, Theon is besieged by Ramsay's northern force. Luwin says twenty men cannot hold it and suggests Theon join the Night's Watch for redemption. Theon refuses, fearing Jon will kill him, and tries to rally his men. They betray him; Dagmer knocks him out. When Luwin intervenes, Dagmer spears him. The ironmen bag Theon and deliver him to the Boltons. Later Bran, Rickon, Osha, and Hodor emerge from the crypts to find Winterfell burning. They find dying Luwin in the godswood. He tells them to go to the Wall; Jon can protect them and tell Catelyn they live. He is proud to have served the Starks and asks Osha for a quick death.

In the Riverlands, Arya, Gendry, and Hot Pie meet Jaqen, who waited for them. He offers to take Arya to Braavos to learn his killing arts. She declines to find her family. He gives her an iron coin and the words "Valar Morghulis" to use with any Braavosi if she needs him. He calls her by her real name and leaves, changing his face.

In Qarth, Daenerys enters the House of the Undying and is separated from Jorah and Kovarro. In a room of many doors she sees visions: the ruined snowy throne room of King's Landing (she turns from the Iron Throne), the Wall, then Drogo's tent with Drogo and infant Rhaego. She leaves after touching the child's hair. Back in the room her dragons are chained. Pyat Pree appears, saying she and they restored his magic, and chains her. Unafraid, she says "Dracarys." The dragons burn him alive. She returns to Xaro's, finds Doreah in bed with him, takes his key, and opens the empty Valyrian stone vault. Something can come from nothing. She locks Xaro and Doreah inside to die and takes enough wealth to buy a ship. The Dothraki seize the rest as she watches with her dragons.

Beyond the Wall, Jon and Qhorin are taken to Mance Rayder's camp by the Lord of Bones. Qhorin sacrifices himself so Jon can pose as a deserter spy. He attacks Jon, insulting his parentage to enrage him. Ygritte returns Jon's sword. Jon kills Qhorin, who whispers "We are the watchers on the Wall" as he dies. The wildlings free Jon, believing he betrayed his oath. Ygritte shows him Mance's vast army in the valley and takes him to their king.

At the Fist of the First Men, Sam, Grenn, and Edd hear three horn blasts—the signal for White Walkers. They run back. Sam is left behind in a sudden blizzard. He hides and watches dozens of wights led by a White Walker on an undead horse pass. The Walker ignores him, raises an ice spear, shrieks, and the army of hundreds advances on the Fist.
